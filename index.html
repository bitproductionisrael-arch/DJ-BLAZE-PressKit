The Artist

The dancefloor meets live music.

DJ BLAZE (Amit Mike Rov) is an Israeli DJ, music producer and live musician working across clubs, beach venues, and stage productions. His sets combine open-format selection with live guitar, MIDI keytar and custom electronic arrangements.

From contemporary House to international favorites. Adaptable programming for venues, festivals and private events.
